# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in its issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is
expected to:

- be respectful and considerate; disagree with ideas, not people;
- give and take review in good faith, on the work and against the stated bar;
- assume competence and good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise;
- keep the project's spaces free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and the
  publication of others' private information.

The maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions and comments that do not meet this,
and may exclude from the project's spaces anyone who repeatedly or seriously breaches it.

To report a breach, write to the maintainers privately through GitHub (a direct message to a
maintainer, or the private vulnerability reporting form for anything that should not be seen by
others). Reports are read by the maintainers alone and answered within a week.
